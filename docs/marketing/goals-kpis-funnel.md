# Marketing goals, KPIs and funnel

Related: #72. Proposed baseline; targets are for the owner to set.

## Funnel

1. Reach: social impressions, Google Business Profile views, QR scans.
2. Visit: Catalogo sessions by UTM (see utm-convention.md).
3. Intent: product views, WhatsApp click-to-chat clicks, "available at store" views.
4. Lead: opted-in contacts with LGPD consent (#74).
5. Sale: sales linked to a campaign (#32, #80).
6. Repeat: customers with 2+ purchases in 90 days.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions by campaign | sessions grouped by `utm_campaign` | analytics |
| WhatsApp conversion | chats started / sessions | analytics click event |
| Lead growth | new opted-in contacts per month | Estoque (#74) |
| Campaign revenue | sale totals with the campaign recorded | Estoque (#80) |
| Promotion lift | units sold in promo period vs previous equal period | Estoque |
| Opt-out rate | opt-outs / broadcasts sent (alert above 2%, assumed) | Estoque (#75) |

Targets: to be set by the owner. Cadence: weekly sessions and WhatsApp conversion; monthly revenue and leads.
